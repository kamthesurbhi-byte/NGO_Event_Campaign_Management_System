Education and Digital Literacy

Community Engagement Project with Sevavrat Foundation


🔺 Observation Activity

Children were given diagrams and asked to count triangles. This activity helped develop analytical and observation skills.

❌ Tic-Tac-Toe

A fun game focused on concentration, quick thinking and decision-making.

🔤 Word Formation

Children formed words using jumbled letters. The activity helped improve vocabulary and language skills.

🔢 Number Completion

Children completed missing numbers to strengthen their number sense and logical thinking.

➕ Mathematics Practice

Addition, subtraction and multiplication exercises were conducted for basic mathematics practice.

🎮 Fun Movement Games

In/Out, Blind Man's Buff and group games were conducted to make learning active and enjoyable.

💻 Computer Introduction

Children were introduced to basic laptop and computer skills to increase awareness of technology.

🎨 Creative Activities

Drawing, painting and craft activities encouraged children to express their imagination and creativity.
