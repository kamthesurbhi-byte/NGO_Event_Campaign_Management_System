About Sevavrat Foundation

Sevavrat Foundation is an NGO working for the welfare of children in slum areas.

The organization focuses on education and overall development of children by creating a supportive learning environment.


Our Community Engagement Project

Our Community Engagement Project (CEP) connected college students with society to develop social responsibility.

We partnered with Sevavrat Foundation to conduct educational and fun-based activities for children from slum communities.


Project Details

Project: Education and Digital Literacy

Duration: 12 Hours across multiple sessions

Beneficiaries: Children from Slum Communities

Focus: Education and Creativity


Learning Activities

Puzzles, mathematics and interactive learning sessions

Drawing, painting and craft activities

Communication, teamwork and leadership activities
